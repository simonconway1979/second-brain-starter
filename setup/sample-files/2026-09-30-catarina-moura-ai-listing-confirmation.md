Joana told me about yesterday's listing review. I have now read PRD v2. As I understand it, the AI draft goes live as soon as the seller taps "List item", and the seller can correct it afterwards.

That design does not meet the advice I gave in July. To be clear about what is needed:

1. **Confirmation comes before publishing.** The seller must see the AI draft and take an active step to confirm it before the listing is live. A correction after publication is too late: the buyer may already have read the listing, and by then it is Rehanga's text, not the seller's. A pre-ticked box does not count either.

2. **Condition and brand need the seller's own confirmation.** A buyer reads these two fields as the seller's statement. If the seller has not confirmed them, we cannot show them as the seller's words.

3. **AI assistance must be labelled.** The label has to be visible to buyers on the listing itself. One short line is enough.

4. **The note should be linked.** The PRD does not link my note of 14 July (Notion, "Legal note: AI-generated content in seller listings"). Please add it, so anyone reading the PRD sees the conditions together with the design.

I understand from Joana that a confirmation step is being designed as a fallback. From a legal point of view it is not a fallback. It is the minimum.
